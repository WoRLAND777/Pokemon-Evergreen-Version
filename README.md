## About

**Pokémon Evergreen Version** is a Pokémon Emerald ROM hack originally created by **Cimbachu888**.

The project was designed around a simple philosophy: expand and personalize Pokémon Emerald without losing what makes the original game feel like Emerald.

Evergreen retains the core story, world, progression, and overall character of the original game while introducing quality-of-life improvements, expanded Pokémon availability, new encounters, restored event content, additional characters, and new postgame material.

Following its original creation, **WoRLAND Studios** handled extensive testing, bug fixing, technical refinement, release preparation, documentation, and publication of the completed project.

## Features

### Expanded Starter Selection

Choose **two starters from six options**:

* Eevee
* Vaporeon
* Jolteon
* Flareon
* Espeon
* Umbreon

---

### Additional Eeveelutions

Later-generation Eeveelutions have been added to Evergreen:

* Leafeon
* Glaceon
* Sylveon

These Pokémon have been incorporated through encounters and events while maintaining Evergreen's Emerald-focused design philosophy.

---

### Expanded Pokémon Encounters

Wild encounters have been reworked throughout Hoenn to make additional Pokémon obtainable while retaining the general structure and feel of Pokémon Emerald's original encounter design.

Rare, unusual, and previously unavailable Pokémon can now be discovered throughout the game.

---

### Increased Shiny Odds

Evergreen features approximately **1.1% shiny odds**.

The final implementation uses natural Pokémon PID generation rather than simply forcing the shiny flag, helping preserve behavior consistent with Generation III Pokémon generation.

---

### Physical / Special Split

Evergreen includes the modern **Physical / Special Split**, allowing individual moves to determine whether they use the Attack or Special Attack stat instead of relying solely on move type.

---

### Fairy Type

The **Fairy type** has been added and integrated into the game.

---

### Restored Emerald Event Content

Pokémon Emerald's event content has been restored and made accessible within Evergreen, including the original event items and associated legendary and mythical encounters.

---

### New Characters and Events

Evergreen includes additional characters, dialogue, encounters, and postgame content while preserving Emerald's original storyline and progression.

---

### Quality-of-Life Improvements

Numerous smaller changes have been made throughout the game to improve Pokémon availability, progression, usability, and general playability without radically restructuring the original Emerald experience.

---

## Design Philosophy

Evergreen was intentionally created as a **mostly vanilla Pokémon Emerald experience with additions rather than a replacement for Emerald**.

The goal is for someone familiar with the original game to immediately recognize Hoenn, its progression, and its story while still regularly discovering something new.

Rather than rebuilding Emerald into an entirely different game, Evergreen expands the existing experience with new Pokémon, encounters, mechanics, events, and personal touches.

## Installation

**Pokémon Evergreen Version is distributed as a patch only.**

No Pokémon Emerald ROM and no pre-patched Evergreen `.gba` file are distributed through this repository.

### Required Base Game

You will need your own clean copy of:

**Pokémon - Emerald Version (USA, Europe)**

### Patch Formats

Evergreen is distributed in:

* **BPS**
* **IPS**

The **BPS patch is recommended** when supported because the format can verify that the expected base ROM is being patched.

### Basic Patching Process

1. Obtain your own clean Pokémon Emerald ROM.
2. Download the latest Evergreen patch from the **Releases** section of this repository.
3. Open a compatible ROM patching utility.
4. Select the Evergreen `.bps` or `.ips` patch.
5. Select your clean Pokémon Emerald ROM as the source.
6. Apply the patch.
7. Load the resulting patched ROM using compatible hardware or an emulator.
